- Terminal-only filtering is represented by terminal_only_filter.

- Adaptive retrieval is represented by adaptive_escalating.
- Action-aware extraction separates restricted-object mention from restricted-action commitment.
- Real masked diffusion model integration is explicitly left for Phase 2.
